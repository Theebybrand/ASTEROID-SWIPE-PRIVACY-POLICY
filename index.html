Privacy Policy for Asteroid Swipe

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Asteroid Swipe is a casual arcade game developed by ASTEROID SWIPE DEVELOPERS.

We respect your privacy. Asteroid Swipe is designed to provide a simple gameplay experience without requiring you to create an account or provide personal information.

Information We Collect

Asteroid Swipe does not collect, request, or store personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, physical address, contacts, photos, or other personally identifiable information.

The game does not require you to create an account or log in.

Game Data

Asteroid Swipe may store game-related information, such as your score or high score, locally on your device so that your progress and best score can be displayed within the game.

This locally stored game information is not transmitted to Asteroid Swipe Developers.

Internet and Data Sharing

Asteroid Swipe is designed to work without an internet connection.

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties.

The game does not use third-party advertising or analytics services.

Permissions

Asteroid Swipe does not require access to sensitive device permissions for its core gameplay.

Data Security

Because Asteroid Swipe does not collect or transmit personal information, no personal information is stored on our servers.

Data Retention and Deletion

Asteroid Swipe does not maintain a personal user database or store personal information on our servers.

Game-related information stored locally on your device can generally be removed by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

Children's Privacy

Asteroid Swipe does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users because the game does not collect personal information.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the features or data practices of Asteroid Swipe change. Any updated version will include a new "Last updated" date.
